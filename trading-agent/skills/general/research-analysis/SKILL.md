# Research Analysis Skill

Framework for conducting thorough, evidence-grounded research across technical documentation, codebases, and quantitative datasets.

## Methodological Steps

1. **Source Discovery & Triangulation**:
   - Collect empirical facts from primary references, official documentation, and runtime telemetry.
   - Cross-verify statements across multiple independent sources before drawing conclusions.

2. **Synthesis & Structuring**:
   - Organize findings into clear hierarchies: Executive Summary, Architectural Implications, Quantitative Metrics, Concrete Recommendations.
   - Distinguish strictly between verified facts, working assumptions, and open risks.

3. **Actionable Deliverables**:
   - Conclude every research cycle with definitive next steps, measurable KPIs, or implementation paths.
