- **Crude Oil (XTIUSD / XBRUSD)**:
  - *Drivers*: Maritime transit risk premiums balanced against OPEC+ quota adjustments and moderated global industrial output.
- **Bitcoin (BTCUSD)**:
  - *Drivers*: Institutional ETF inflows and sovereign/corporate balance sheet adoption, coupled with sensitivity to global M2 liquidity expansion.
